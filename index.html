    How to play

    1. Animated: Mr. Wiggles moves with the crowd. He may slip behind a kiosk or pillar, but always reappears.
    2. Classic Mode: search five original, densely composed still illustrations. People, rides and vehicles stay frozen; the clock, camera and hints still work. Turn it on in Settings.
    3. Pinch or scroll to zoom, drag to look around.
    4. Tap him when you spot him. Tapping the wrong person counts as a wrong guess.
    5. Stuck? A Hint circles the area he's in, but costs you a star.
    6. Keyboard: arrows or WASD to pan (move the crosshair at picture edges), +/− to zoom, Enter to check the crosshair.

    Find him at the station, airport, Galleria, Market and Fairground. Finish a scene to unlock the next in that mode, or replay completed scenes for a new hiding place. Animated and Classic progress are saved separately on this device. No accounts needed.
